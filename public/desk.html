ASSIGNMENT DESK

← back to the broadcast

Give the cast something to talk about: an idea, a link to an article, or both. With a link, the station reads the page and the cast discusses the real story; a fact-checker cuts anything the article doesn't support. Pick a show or let whichever show is on air take it.

    VIEWER MAIL

    Messages viewers send from the main page. The local model screens them automatically; anything it couldn't check waits here for you. Approved mail gets read on air in The Late Byte, Rise & Pixel and Couch Co-op.

      MUSICAL GUEST

      Put a band on right after the current scene; whoever is hosting introduces them. Bands perform your own tracks if you add them to data/music/<band>/ (add "Song.vocals.mp3" for exact lip-sync); otherwise they play an original song generated live.

      SPECIAL PROGRAMMING

      Break into the schedule: put any show on air. With "cut in" checked it starts right after the scene that's playing now, skipping anything already queued; unchecked, queued scenes play first. The regular schedule resumes when it ends.
